## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a tactile, high-end editorial atmosphere tailored for a curated maker marketplace. It honors human craft, slow living, and provenance through quiet precision and literary elegance. The aesthetic merges gallery minimalism with tactile warmth, prioritizing ample negative space, intentional asymmetry, and deliberate typographic rhythm over transactional clutter.

The interface serves design-conscious patrons, collectors, and independent artisans. It evokes the feeling of thumbing through a heavyweight independent quarterly publication—grounded, serene, tactile, and intellectually honest. 

Key stylistic tenets:
- **Quiet Authority:** Ample breathing room, generous margins, and deliberate editorial pacing eliminate market noise.
- **Material Realism:** Flat, cold digital surfaces are replaced with warm, fibrous tones, soft hairline borders, and subtle structural frames that echo fine bookbinding and archival matting.
- **Provocative Typographic Contrast:** A stately, high-contrast transitional serif commands editorial discovery, paired with an understated, highly functional geometric grotesque for uninterrupted functional clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in elemental earth materials—clay, pine needles, raw unbleached linen, and river silt. Contrast is maintained strictly within natural spectrums to prevent visual fatigue while exceeding WCAG AA legibility criteria across all interactive layers.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#B96345` / Terracotta):** Used for focal action points, verified artisan signatures, interactive hover states, and primary checkout pathways. Represents hand-turned earthenware.
- **Secondary (`#294C3A` / Deep Forest Green):** The commanding anchor. Utilized for display typography, formal high-contrast headers, inverted editorial sections, and structural accents. Delivers botanical gravitas.
- **Tertiary (`#D9C5A5` / Sand):** The bridging tint. Applied to tactile card frames, quiet borders, secondary chip containers, and subtle warm backdrops.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F7F3EB` / Ivory):** The atmospheric base. Replaces stark digital white with the organic warmth of archival vellum and raw cotton paper.
- **Body Neutral (`#262320` / Warm Charcoal):** A rich, organic off-black tinted with umber. Ensures extreme reading comfort without the clinical harshness of `#000000`.

### Functional Application
- Primary buttons strictly pair `#B96345` with `#F7F3EB` text.
- Secondary structural buttons utilize `#294C3A` with `#F7F3EB` text for high-importance provenance storytelling.
- Card and surface borders strictly rely on `#D9C5A5` at 70%–100% opacity, never defaulting to cold slate or pure grey dividers.

## Typography

The type system creates an intentional tension between classical literary grace and functional modern restraint. 

### Editorial Display Hierarchy
Headlines employ **Playfair Display**, evoking archival curation, traditional letterpress titling, and high-fashion editorial spreads.
- Large display scales favor italicization for narrative accent words (e.g., *Handmade*, *Origins*, *Ceramics*).
- Letter spacing is intentionally tightened at scales above 32px to ensure cohesive, dignified word groupings.

### Interface & Utility Hierarchy
Functional microcopy, item descriptions, metadata, and commerce mechanics rely on **Plus Jakarta Sans**.
- Body text retains relaxed leading (`1.625rem` on `1rem` base) to optimize editorial readability against warm ivory backgrounds.
- All uppercase taglines, artisan origin stamps, and badge identifiers use `label-sm` or `label-md` with expanded letter-spacing (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to mimic stamped textile marks.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a disciplined, asymmetrical grid system inspired by Swiss book design and physical art gallery catalogues. Instead of dense commercial item walls, modules are grouped with deliberate cadence and expansive negative space.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column fluid grid constrained to a maximum width of `1440px`. Gutter is `2rem` (`gutter-lg`), margin is `4rem` (`margin-lg`).
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `1.5rem` (`gutter`) and `2rem` outer page margins (`margin`).
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4-column grid with `1rem` gutters (`gutter-sm`) and `1rem` outer horizontal margins (`margin-sm`).

### Rhythm & Composition
- Layout spacing follows an organic 8px baseline rhythm, with generous macroscopic section separation (`space-xl` scaled dynamically up to `5rem` for chapter breaks).
- Product cards alternate between standard vertical proportions and offset editorial landscape blocks, encouraging thoughtful browsing rather than rapid pagination.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through tactile surface layering, soft paper textures, and warm atmospheric shadows rather than artificial digital elevation tiers.

### Structural Depth Techniques
- **Surface Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#F7F3EB` (Ivory). Raised containers (cards, flyout trays, popovers) use crisp, bright warm whites (`#FCFAF7`) bordered with `#D9C5A5`. Inset wells and search inputs adopt `#EFE9DC`.
- **Warm Umber Shadows:** Cast shadows strictly avoid cold black or blue tints. Shadows utilize a 10%–14% mix of warm raw umber (`rgba(41, 35, 29, 0.08)`) with generous blur radii (`16px` to `32px`) and zero hard spread, replicating diffused, natural daylight through linen windows.
- **Framed Matting:** Prominent collection showcases and artisan profile cards use hairline `1px` borders in `#D9C5A5` with `0.5rem` internal margin offsets, creating an archival framing effect.

## Shapes

The shape architecture is defined as **Soft (Level 1)**, reflecting hand-trimmed cardstock, carved wood, and architectural masonry. Excessive rounding or circular UI elements are avoided to maintain an authentic, handcrafted feel.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Geometry:** Standard components (inputs, actionable buttons, cards, drop-downs) apply a restrained `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
- **Large Assemblies:** Modals, hero image containers, and floating panels use `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Badges & Handcrafted Tags:** Distinctive tag components utilize clipped angles or subtle `0.125rem` corners to evoke stamped cloth tags and leather labels.
- **Natural Language Discovery Bar:** Combines a soft `0.375rem` wrapper with crisp internal segment dividers to anchor the search experience with architectural presence.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Triggers
- **Primary Action (Terracotta):** Solid `#B96345` background, `#F7F3EB` text, `0.25rem` border radius, padding `0.75rem 1.75rem`. Hover state transitions to deep umber-terracotta (`#A25337`) with a soft umber ambient shadow.
- **Secondary Action (Forest Archive):** Deep `#294C3A` background with `#F7F3EB` text for high-impact provenance pathways and collection stories.
- **Editorial Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px` border in `#294C3A` or `#B96345`, text matching the border color. On hover, fills with a 6% wash of the border tone.

### Natural Language Discovery Bar
- Centered, expansive search canvas spanning 8 to 10 desktop columns.
- Surface: `#FCFAF7` surrounded by a `1px` border in `#D9C5A5`.
- Text input displays stylized editorial placeholder prompts (e.g., *"Find wheel-thrown stoneware mugs from coastal Oregon..."*).
- Trailing search action is enclosed in a minimal Terracotta pill with an organic icon and `label-md` typographic styling.

### Cards (Artisan & Product)
- **Background:** `#FCFAF7` with a delicate `1px` `#D9C5A5` solid border.
- **Imagery:** Aspect ratios fixed to 4:5 (portrait) or 1:1 (square), framed with an inner `0.25rem` paper-mat margin.
- **Content Flow:** Features a small caps provenance tag (`label-sm`), followed by the product name in `Playfair Display` (`title-lg`), closing with price and artisan name in `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`body-sm`).

### Artisan Badges & Handcrafted Tags
- **Artisan Provenance Badge:** Circular or rectangular textile-inspired label using `#294C3A` background, `#F7F3EB` uppercase lettering, accompanied by a verified maker glyph.
- **Process Chips (e.g., "Hand-Thrown", "Woodfired", "Zero Waste"):** `#EFE9DC` surface, `1px` hairline border in `#D9C5A5`, `0.25rem` radius, text set in `#294C3A` at `label-md`.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Inputs:** `#FCFAF7` base with a warm `1px` border in `#D9C5A5`. On focus, the border transitions cleanly to `#B96345` with a subtle `2px` focus ring in `rgba(185, 99, 69, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Hand-finished square geometry (`0.125rem` radius) for checkboxes; circular rings for radios. Checked state fills with `#294C3A` exhibiting a clean `#F7F3EB` checkmark icon.

### Editorial Story Section (Django Showcase Module)
- Split narrative module coupling high-resolution artisan studio photography with rich-text Django CMS fields.
- Background defaults to `#EFE9DC` or deep `#294C3A` with inverted Ivory text, incorporating block quotes formatted in `Playfair Display` italic.
